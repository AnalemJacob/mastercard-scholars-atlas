Which information do I need?

Required: a unique id and name. Include country_code (ISO3, e.g. GHA), hometown, region, latitude and longitude to map a hometown.

Add university, programme, cohort, theme, bio, aspiration, photo, audio, source_url and location_verified as available. Missing coordinates are allowed; those scholars still count in country totals.

Use scholar-approved town centres. A hometown is the place a scholar identifies as home; it may differ from citizenship. This atlas does not infer an individual's admissions eligibility.

To update the map for every visitor, replace the project's data/scholars.json with your export and republish. Your original CSV remains unchanged.
BEHIND THE MAP

A community, seen through place.

An independent, scholar-created exploration of the Mastercard Foundation Scholars Program. The directory includes publicly documented 2026 scholars from several universities; coverage is partial. It is not an official Foundation directory.

Reading the numbers

Country shares count unique scholars in the selected university and cohort. Searching, selecting a country, and moving the map do not change that denominator. Missing home countries appear in a separate category. A zero means no records in this dataset; it does not establish that a country has no scholars.

Country eligibility reference

The geographic reference is UBC · Winter 2027. UBC states that applicants must be a citizen or refugee of an African country, alongside academic, age, degree-programme and other conditions.

The 54-country layer implements that geographic rule using African UN member states. It is not an enumerated country list published by UBC. Western Sahara is shown as unverified. The reference remains UBC-specific when viewing records from other universities.

Reference checked: 24 October 2026. A country highlight is not a personal eligibility decision.

Map and data

Country boundaries: Natural Earth, public domain. Detailed street map: OpenFreeMap, OpenMapTiles and OpenStreetMap contributors, with an Esri street-map fallback for browsers without WebGL. Satellite imagery: Esri and its credited imagery providers. Town coverage and spelling vary by location. Until hometowns are confirmed, points use Natural Earth country label locations as illustrative country references. No country reference is stored as a scholar’s hometown coordinate. Connections show country–UBC relationships rather than travel itineraries.

Built for Analem Jacob's scholar-community mapping project. The directory combines the supplied UBC roster with named 2026 profiles from official university sources. Cambridge uses the academic-year label 2026/2027. Country origins are left blank when not confirmed. Connections follow each scholar’s university. Destination points are representative university locations, not individual study buildings. Scholars without a recorded home country have no connection line. Professional profiles and photographs are included only for corroborated public matches. Hometown mapping is deferred.
